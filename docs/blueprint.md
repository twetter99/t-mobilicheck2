# **App Name**: T-MobiliCheck

## Core Features:

- Offline Data Entry: Allows technicians to fill out maintenance checklists and capture data even without an active internet connection. Data is stored locally and synchronized when connectivity is restored.
- Dynamic Form Generation: Dynamically generates maintenance checklists based on the type of bus and previous maintenance history, ensuring the technician is presented with the relevant items.
- AI-Powered Data Validation Tool: Uses a tool to check entered values in the maintenance checklists against expected ranges and historical data to identify potential errors or anomalies, alerting the technician to double-check the values.
- Photo and QR/Barcode Capture: Enables technicians to take photos of equipment and scan QR/barcodes to automatically populate serial numbers and other equipment data.
- Signature Capture: Allows technicians and supervisors to add their signatures directly on the screen to approve the maintenance checklists.
- Automated PDF Generation: Automatically generates a standardized PDF report of the completed maintenance checklist, including photos, signatures, and all entered data, ready for submission to the operator.
- Corrective Action Trigger: If an issue is detected during the preventive maintenance, the app automatically triggers the creation of a corrective maintenance order, ensuring timely follow-up and resolution.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep sky blue (#3498DB) to reflect trust, reliability, and technical expertise.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F3F4) for a clean and professional look.
- Accent color: Orange (#E67E22) to highlight important actions and alerts.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a sans-serif font, providing a modern and objective look. Note: currently only Google Fonts are supported.
- Use clear and recognizable icons for navigation and common actions. Icons should be consistent with the app's overall style.
- A card-based layout for presenting checklist items and form fields in a clear and organized manner. Prioritize a single-column layout for mobile screens.
- Subtle animations for transitions and feedback. Avoid distracting or unnecessary animations.
